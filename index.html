METHOD / NOT A GUARANTEE

Measure the constraint, not the label.

  1. 01

    Use the real desk edge.

    A listed desk size can omit trim, wall gaps, or the space occupied by a monitor foot.

  2. 02

    Record mounting obstructions.

    Rear aprons, bevels, cable trays, walls, and fixed hardware can matter as much as thickness.

  3. 03

    Open the exact manual.

    Compare your card with the current dimensions and compatibility notes for the exact model and variant.

  4. 04

    Treat the answer as conditional.

    The card narrows a physical-fit question. It does not guarantee installation, comfort, stability, or performance.

SOURCE NOTE / CHECKED 2026-08-08

Background, not borrowed certainty.

The four-field checklist is an original Small Desk Lab planning method. OSHA and CCOHS support considering workspace depth, equipment, movement, and clear space. They do not say these four measurements guarantee a product will fit.
